Accuracy matters more. Equity matters — AI that only works for the median user is failing the people who need help most. Heavy bias toward AI augmenting humans (teachers, coaches, counselors, clinicians) rather than replacing them.

On rollout: Pilot before scale. Define success metrics before you build. Track them. Iterate. Kill what doesn't work. Don't fall in love with the demo.
